## Programs

2 laws, all of them IEC 61131-3 Structured Text compiled by matiec.
The roster is `tables/programs.csv` and it decides the order they run
in; `BotanikFirmware` owns the resource that dispatches them, one task
each. A law reads and writes rows BY NAME, and nothing is passed
between them.

| # | program | rate | reads | writes | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | vent | control | 5 | 8 | The whole site: pick the warmer level, work out a target, and open the vent in proportion to how far over it the air is. |
| 2 | water | control | 5 | 3 | The tank's running total, integrated from its flow meter. |

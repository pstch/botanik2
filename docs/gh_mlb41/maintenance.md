# Maintenance {#gh_mlb41_maintenance}

<!-- From the spec's "Maintenance". -->

## Regular tasks

- **At least every three months**, start the generator and let it run for at
  least 30 minutes. That also confirms the actuators still work when they are
  running on it.
- **At least once a month**, and rather more in summer, water the plants from
  above, by sprinkling. Sub-irrigation accumulates salts and particles at the
  surface, and this is what clears them.
- **At least every two months**, check the residual-current devices trip. That
  also confirms the control system behaves correctly when it loses its supply
  entirely.
- **Regularly**, read the recent logs and look at the graphs, to confirm the
  control system is doing what it should.
